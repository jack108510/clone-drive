Questions?

What if my computer dies?

Your CloneDrive has a full copy. Plug it into any Windows PC and your files are right there. The 1TB version is even bootable — you can start your computer directly from the drive.

Is it really that easy?

Yes. Plug it in, double-click "Back Up My PC", type YES. That's it. No software to download, no setup wizard, no tech knowledge needed.

How long does a backup take?

Depends on how much is on your computer. Most people are done in 30 minutes to 2 hours. You can keep using your computer while it backs up.

Can I back up more than one computer?

Yes. Each backup is saved in a separate folder with the date and computer name. You can back up your whole family's computers on one drive.

What's the difference between the sizes?

64GB covers documents and photos. 256GB backs up most full PCs. 1TB creates a complete bootable clone — if your PC dies, you can boot straight from the drive and everything works.
